# 英语精读训练：零基础偏弱版

> 目标：先把句子看懂，再去做阅读。  
> 规则：每天 3 句即可，吃力时只做前 2 句。不要追求快，先追求不断。  
> 译文已单独放在 `英语精读训练-答案.md`，先拆完当天句子再打开答案。

## 一、每天怎么练

每天流程固定，不用重新规划。

| 顺序 | 任务 | 要求 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 背单词 | 用你自己的背词软件，按软件计划走。 |
| 2 | 精读句子 | 每天 3 句，吃力就 2 句。 |
| 3 | 复盘 | 把今天最不会的 3 个词和 1 个句子抄下来。 |

建议时间：

| 状态 | 时间 |
|---|---|
| 普通日 | 30-45 分钟 |
| 低状态日 | 15-20 分钟 |
| 周末加强 | 60 分钟 |

## 二、精读一句话的 6 步

先不要追求优美翻译。你现在只要能回答：

```text
谁？
做什么？
对谁/什么？
补充说明是什么？
```

### 第 1 步：先读一遍

不要一上来查词。先读一遍，感受大概。

### 第 2 步：圈出动词

动词是句子的心脏。优先找这些：

```text
is / are / was / were
do / does / did
have / has / had
make / take / find / become / help / need / affect
```

### 第 3 步：找主语

问自己：谁做这个动作？谁是什么？

例如：

```text
Many students find math difficult.
```

```text
动词：find
主语：Many students
```

### 第 4 步：找宾语或表语

问自己：做了什么？影响了什么？是什么样？

```text
Many students find math difficult.
```

```text
宾语：math
补充：difficult
意思：许多学生觉得数学难。
```

### 第 5 步：处理修饰成分

看到这些，先当成“补充说明”：

```text
of ...
in ...
with ...
because ...
when ...
which ...
who ...
that ...
```

不要怕长句。长句通常只是短句外面挂了很多补充说明。

### 第 6 步：翻译成人话

不要逐词硬翻。先把意思说通顺。

## 四、示范拆句

### 示例 1

```text
Learning English takes time.
```

```text
动词：takes
主语：Learning English
宾语：time
翻译：学习英语需要时间。
```

### 示例 2

```text
I often forget words that I learned yesterday.
```

```text
动词：forget / learned
主语：I
宾语：words
修饰：that I learned yesterday 修饰 words
翻译：我经常忘记昨天学过的单词。
```

### 示例 3

```text
Although the sentence is long, its meaning is simple.
```

```text
主句动词：is
主句主语：its meaning
从句：Although the sentence is long
翻译：虽然这个句子很长，但它的意思很简单。
```

## 五、30 天句子训练表

说明：

- 每天 3 句。
- 第 1 句必须做。
- 第 2 句尽量做。
- 第 3 句有余力再做。
- 答案在单独文件里，先不要打开。

---

### Day 1：最基础的主谓宾

1. Learning English takes time.  

2. Good habits change our lives.  

3. Many students need more practice.  

---

### Day 2：主语可以是一件事

1. Reading helps students learn faster.  

2. Making mistakes is normal.  

3. Solving problems can build confidence.  

---

### Day 3：主系表

1. The question is simple.  

2. The answer seems clear.  

3. The process becomes easier with practice.  

---

### Day 4：常见动词 find / make

1. I find this sentence difficult.  

2. Practice makes the method useful.  

3. Many people find it hard to stay focused.  

---

### Day 5：时间状语

1. I review old words every morning.  

2. She studies math after dinner.  

3. Students often feel tired at night.  

---

### Day 6：地点和方式

1. He writes notes in a notebook.  

2. We learn better in a quiet room.  

3. She explains the idea in simple words.  

---

### Day 7：复习日

1. A slow start is still a start.  

2. Small steps can lead to big changes.  

3. Review is more important than speed.  

---

### Day 8：不定式 to do

1. I want to improve my English.  

2. It is hard to remember every word.  

3. The best way to learn is to practice.  

---

### Day 9：动名词 doing

1. Reading aloud helps pronunciation.  

2. Taking notes can improve memory.  

3. Learning without review is easy to forget.  

---

### Day 10：形容词修饰名词

1. A difficult sentence needs careful reading.  

2. Simple methods are often useful.  

3. A clear plan reduces fear.  

---

### Day 11：of 结构

1. The meaning of the word is clear.  

2. The end of the story surprised me.  

3. The habit of daily review is important.  

---

### Day 12：介词短语

1. The book on the desk is mine.  

2. The girl with long hair is my classmate.  

3. The problem in this chapter is not easy.  

---

### Day 13：because 原因

1. I study every day because I want to change.  

2. He failed the test because he did not review.  

3. Many students feel nervous because the exam is important.  

---

### Day 14：when 时间从句

1. I feel better when I finish my task.  

2. People learn more when they ask questions.  

3. A word becomes familiar when you see it many times.  

---

### Day 15：although 转折

1. Although English is hard, I can improve.  

2. Although the passage is short, it contains many new words.  

3. Although progress is slow, it is still progress.  

---

### Day 16：that 宾语从句

1. I know that practice is necessary.  

2. She believes that effort can change results.  

3. Many students think that grammar is difficult.  

---

### Day 17：who 定语从句

1. A student who reviews often remembers more.  

2. People who read every day improve slowly but surely.  

3. A teacher who explains clearly can reduce confusion.  

---

### Day 18：which 定语从句

1. The book which I bought yesterday is useful.  

2. The method which he uses is simple.  

3. The sentence which looks long may be easy.  

---

### Day 19：被动语态

1. The word is used in many passages.  

2. The problem was solved by another method.  

3. Important ideas are often repeated in exams.  

---

### Day 20：比较级

1. Reading is more useful than guessing.  

2. Review is better than learning many new words at once.  

3. A clear sentence is easier to understand than a long but unclear one.  

---

### Day 21：it 作形式主语

1. It is important to keep studying.  

2. It is normal to feel slow at the beginning.  

3. It is useful to read the sentence twice.  

---

### Day 22：there be 句型

1. There are many ways to learn English.  

2. There is no need to be perfect every day.  

3. There are several reasons for this change.  

---

### Day 23：not only ... but also

1. English needs not only memory but also practice.  

2. Reading improves not only vocabulary but also thinking.  

3. A good plan saves not only time but also energy.  

---

### Day 24：so ... that

1. The sentence is so long that I feel afraid.  

2. The word appears so often that I remember it.  

3. The explanation is so clear that many students understand it.  

---

### Day 25：考研阅读常见表达

1. Education plays an important role in society.  

2. Technology has changed the way people communicate.  

3. The author discusses the influence of modern life.  

---

### Day 26：观点和态度

1. Some people believe that success depends on talent.  

2. Others argue that effort is more important.  

3. The writer seems to support the second view.  

---

### Day 27：原因和结果

1. Stress can affect a student's performance.  

2. Lack of sleep may lead to poor memory.  

3. Regular practice helps people deal with difficult tasks.  

---

### Day 28：转折和让步

1. The method is simple, but it works well.  

2. The result was disappointing, yet he continued to study.  

3. Although many problems remain, progress has been made.  

---

### Day 29：稍长一点的句子

1. Students who review their notes regularly are more likely to remember key ideas.  

2. When people face a difficult task, they often need a clear plan.  

3. The habit of reading a few sentences every day can make English less frightening.  

---

### Day 30：阶段检测

1. What matters most is not speed, but consistency.  

2. A person who keeps learning will understand more than a person who only worries.  

3. If you can read one sentence carefully, you can slowly learn to read a passage.

## 六、做完 30 天以后怎么办

做完后不要急着换很难的材料。按这个顺序来：

| 阶段 | 任务 |
|---|---|
| 第 1 步 | 把这 30 天里最难的 30 句重新拆一遍。 |
| 第 2 步 | 开始每天精读 1 小段英文，长度 3-5 句。 |
| 第 3 步 | 开始做考研阅读真题，但不限时间，以看懂为主。 |

## 七、你现在最该避免的事

- 不要一天背很多单词，然后完全不复习。
- 不要一上来硬刷整篇考研阅读。
- 不要只看中文翻译，觉得自己看懂了。
- 不要因为读得慢就否定自己。
- 不要天天换方法。

真正有用的是：

```text
每天背词
每天拆句
每周复盘
长期不断
```

英语差不是判决书。现在先从一句一句读懂开始。
